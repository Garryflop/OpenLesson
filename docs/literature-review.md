# Literature review

16 sources reviewed (required: at least 12), of them 14 scientific articles in Scopus Q1-Q3 journals (required: at least 8).

**Search and selection:** Scopus-indexed journals (found through OpenAlex and publisher sites), 2020-2026, higher
education, free full text only. Keywords: peer observation of teaching, teacher PD platform, LLM feedback analysis.

## Themes

| Theme [sources] | What the literature agrees on | What is missing | Decision for OpenLesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peer observation of teaching [1-4] | Developmental, transparent, collegial models work. Feedback needs strengths, weaknesses and comments | Digital tools for the whole cycle. Only 3 of 13 forms are validated | Status-based cycle, configurable rubric, teacher reply (M1-M3, M6) |
| PD platforms [5-8] | Dashboards and history support growth. Speed and service drive satisfaction | Platforms for university staff, not school teachers | Teacher cabinet and dashboard, SUS 70 or higher, WCAG 2.1 AA (M8) |
| PD adoption [9-11] | Usefulness and ease of use decide uptake. Unclear schedules and tech issues hurt | Acceptance data for peer-observation tools | Reminders 10 / 7 / 3 days, UTAUT survey in the pilot (M2) |
| AI on feedback [12-14] | LLMs reach human-level agreement on themes and cut analysis time about 2.5 times | Use on peer-observation feedback and non-English text | AI summary with quotes, always checked by a person (M7) |
| Client spec and standards [15-16] | 8 modules, 5 roles, 10 / 7 / 3-day cycle. WCAG 2.1 accessibility levels | Feedback criteria are not listed in the spec | Spec is the main source of requirements |

**Evidence base:** 14 journal articles (7 Q1, 3 Q1-Q2, 1 Q2, 2 Q2-Q3, 1 Q3): 3 reviews, 5 survey or interview studies,
2 design studies, 1 course audit and 3 AI text-analysis studies, plus the client specification and WCAG 2.1.

**Research gap:** every building block is studied separately. No source joins scheduling, deadline control,
attendance, peer feedback and AI summaries for open lessons at a university. OpenLesson fills this gap.
